What is being purchased

Team and Business run in your environment. Your identity provider, organization records and signing keys remain under your control. Registration allowances apply locally to each configured organization. Receipt verification does not consume an allowance.

Paddle handles purchaser details and payment. A local purchase key controls software-license requests; no MFENX account is required. Monthly subscriptions require an online refresh of their time-bounded software license. Model files, evaluation data and receipt history are not part of that exchange.

Private deployment uses a time-bounded offline license. Disconnected receipt checks do not contact a billing service. OEM distribution is separately licensed according to the integration and redistribution scope.
